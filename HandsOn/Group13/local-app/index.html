How the application connects the data (RDF / SPARQL)

The chosen facility leads to its district, its Wikidata identity, bordering districts, and their parking observations. SPARQL selects and averages the observations. The same values feed the cards and map.

Facility → local district → owl:sameAs → Wikidata district → P47 border → other district → parking → monthly observations
