3. KEY FINDINGS & CIVIC DOMAIN INSIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The 190 violations revealed key real-world civic domain realities where our
theoretical ontology assumptions conflicted with municipal geography:

Finding A: Intersection Coordinates & Multi-Label Addresses
- In our Knowledge Graph, location URIs are minted based on unique coordinates:
  res:Location/{Latitude},{Longitude}
- At street intersections (e.g., 34.0785565615, -118.261545738), multiple civic
  tickets occurred at the exact same physical coordinates.
- Ticket 1 recorded the intersection as: "LIBERTY ST AT RESERVOIR ST"
- Ticket 2 recorded the intersection as: "RESERVOIR ST AT LIBERTY ST"
- Insight: A physical intersection legitimately has multiple address descriptions
  and multiple intersecting street names. Restricting locations to sh:maxCount 1
  for address and streetName was an ontological over-constraint.

Finding B: Multi-Jurisdictional Administrative Regions
- Administrative regions in LA 311 are composite zones identified by {NC}_{CD}
  (Neighborhood Council and Council District).
- Example: res:AdministrativeRegion/64_4 ("Bel Air-Beverly Crest CD 4").
- This region spans TWO distinct Area Planning Commissions:
  * "Central APC"
  * "West Los Angeles APC"
- Similarly, administrative regions frequently cross multiple LAPD Police
  Precinct boundaries (e.g., Hollywood vs. West LA).
- Insight: Imposing sh:maxCount 1 on apc and policePrecinct contradicted the
  geographical reality of Los Angeles city administrative boundaries.


4. FIXES APPLIED TO SHACL SHAPES ("AFTER")
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In accordance with the assignment guidelines ("fix any problem found during validation"),
we refined shacl/shapes.ttl:

1. Relaxed Over-Constrained Cardinalities:
   - Removed sh:maxCount 1 from la311:address and la311:streetName.
   - Removed sh:maxCount 1 from la311:apc and la311:policePrecinct.
   - Removed sh:maxCount 1 from la311:approximateAddress and la311:houseNumber.

2. Preserved Strict Quality Safeguards:
   - Kept mandatory checks intact: sh:minCount 1 on la311:address (a location
     must always have at least one address label).
   - Kept mandatory checks on la311:apc and la311:policePrecinct (sh:minCount 1).
   - Maintained strict datatype enforcement (xsd:string, xsd:decimal, geo:wktLiteral).
   - Maintained IRI object constraints on external links (owl:sameAs, cdMember).
